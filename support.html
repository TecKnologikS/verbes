Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my “Remove ads” purchase?

Open Settings (the gear button at the top of the screen), then tap “Restore purchase”, signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. If you bought “Remove ads” in an earlier version of the app, it is the same purchase and it is restored the same way. The message “No purchase to restore for this account.” means the App Store found no purchase for this Apple Account.

I bought “Remove ads” but I still see ads

Restore the purchase as described above, with an internet connection. Once the purchase is recognised, Settings shows a thank-you message instead of the purchase button, and ads disappear.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If the purchase is refunded, ads come back.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: Settings → “Ad privacy”. For tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do review reminders work?

The app sends at most one reminder a day, at the time you choose (6 pm by default), only when you have reviews to do, with their number. iOS asks for permission after your first completed session. In Settings → Reminder, turn “Daily reminder” on or off and choose the “Reminder time”. If notifications are turned off for the app, tap “Open iOS Settings” and allow them.

How do I hear the pronunciation?

Tap a verb in the list to hear its three forms, or use the listen buttons on the verb page and in exercises. To choose an American or British voice, go to Settings → English → American or British: the forms read and the voice follow this choice. Pronunciation works even in silent mode; if you hear nothing, turn the volume up.

How do I change the app’s language?

Settings → Language: English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano or Português. The language is used for the app and for the translations of verbs and examples. On first launch, the app uses your phone’s language if it is one of these six, otherwise English.

Will I keep my progress?

Yes when you update the app, including from earlier versions. There is no account or sync: your progress stays on this device and only moves to a new one if you restore a backup of the old one (iCloud or computer). Deleting the app deletes your progress.
