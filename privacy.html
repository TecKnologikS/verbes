Privacy policy

Last updated: [À compléter : date]

In short: Irregular Verbs has no user account and we have no server: your progress, your list and your settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect data from your device under Google’s rules; buying “Remove ads” removes them. The app contains no analytics and no crash reporting tool.

2. Data the app uses

Your learning data

The app records, on your device only: your progress on each verb (review stage and date of the last review), your custom list, the history of your answers (verb, type of exercise, right or wrong, date), your best Sprint score and your settings (app language, American or British English, level studied, daily reminder and its time, whether “Remove ads” was bought, whether the first-launch steps are done).

This data is stored in the app’s database (Core Data) and preferences (iOS user defaults). It is never sent to us: there is no account and no server for the app. The app does not sync it with iCloud; like any app data, it may be included in the backups of your device that you make with iCloud or a computer, under Apple’s rules. Deleting the app erases it.

Pronunciation

Verbs and example sentences are read by the speech synthesis built into iOS, on your device, with an American or British English voice depending on your choice. The app sends the text read to no service.

Daily reminder

Reminders are local notifications: the app schedules them on your device, at most one a day at the time you choose, and only when reviews are due. No notification comes from a server, and the app uses no push notification service. iOS asks for your permission after your first completed session, or when you turn the reminder on. You can turn it off in the app (Settings → Reminder) or in iOS Settings.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) in the verb list, on the verb page and on the Progress screen, never during exercises. The first time you open one of these screens (never at launch or during the first-launch steps):

  • in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent tool (User Messaging Platform) asks for your consent to personalised ads. You can change or withdraw it at any time in the app: Settings → “Ad privacy”;
  • then iOS asks whether you allow the app to track your activity (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app or to Google. You can change your choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

No ad is requested before this step, nor if the consent tool does not allow it. Ad requests contain none of your learning data (progress, answers, list).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect data from your device, such as the advertising identifier (only if you allowed tracking), the IP address, information about the device and the app, and your interactions with ads. Google processes this data under its own rules: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage ad personalisation in My Ad Center. The app also declares the SKAdNetwork identifiers of ad networks: this Apple mechanism lets them measure installs that follow an ad without identifying you.

Your consent choice is stored on your device by Google’s consent tool.

“Remove ads” purchase

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase made through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple handles payment under its own privacy policy: the app never sees your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether this purchase exists for your Apple Account, and keeps on your device the fact that it was bought. Once it is bought, ads are no longer shown or requested, and on later launches Google’s ads SDK is not started at all.

What we don’t do

  • No account, no sign-up, no email requested.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting and no trackers of our own.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • This website uses no cookies, loads no external resources and runs no scripts.
