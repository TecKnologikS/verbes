Free version

The app is free. The free version shows ads, as described in the privacy policy.

“Remove ads” purchase

“Remove ads” is a one-time (non-consumable) purchase, with no subscription. It removes ads from the app; all other features are the same in both versions. Its price is shown in the App Store before purchase. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account and can be restored from the app (Settings → “Restore purchase”). Refund requests follow Apple’s rules (reportaproblem.apple.com); if Apple refunds or revokes the purchase, ads appear again.
